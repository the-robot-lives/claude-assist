# US-407: Preview Who Will See a Post Before Publishing

## User Story

**As a** Creator
**I want to** see a summary of my expected audience before I hit Publish
**So that** I can confirm the reach matches my intent

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I have composed a post with tags and an audience setting
  **When** I tap "Preview Audience"
  **Then** I see estimated counts: direct mutuals, interest-filtered reach at 2nd/3rd degree, and channel subscribers

- **Given** I review the audience preview
  **When** I adjust the audience setting
  **Then** the preview updates in real-time to reflect the new configuration

## Notes
Counts are estimates, not exact figures, for privacy reasons.
