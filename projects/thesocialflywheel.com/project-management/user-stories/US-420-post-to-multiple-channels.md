# US-420: Post to Multiple Interest Channels Simultaneously

## User Story

**As a** Bridge-Builder
**I want to** publish a single post to multiple interest channels at once
**So that** content relevant to overlapping communities reaches all of them without duplicating effort

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am composing a post
  **When** I select up to three channels from the channel picker
  **Then** the post appears as a single entry in each selected channel's feed

- **Given** a post has been sent to multiple channels
  **When** a user views it in any channel
  **Then** a label shows "Also in: [other channels]"

## Notes
Posting to more than three channels requires a Creator-tier account.
