# US-577: Amplify a Post with Commentary

## User Story

**As a** Bridge-Builder
**I want to** amplify a post with my own framing
**So that** I can contextualise content for my web while crediting the original poster

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I select "Quote & Amplify" on a post
  **When** I add my framing text and publish
  **Then** my quoted post is broadcast to my web with my commentary above the embedded original

- **Given** my amplification is published
  **Then** it appears as a standalone post on my profile and in the feeds of my mutuals who follow the channel

## Notes
Distinguishable from a plain repost (US-556) by showing visible commentary. Quote length capped at the standard post character limit.
