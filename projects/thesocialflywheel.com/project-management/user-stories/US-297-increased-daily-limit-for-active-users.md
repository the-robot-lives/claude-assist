# US-297: Increased Daily Limit for Active Users

## User Story

**As a** Niche Enthusiast (P-002)
**I want to** earn a higher daily swipe limit based on my activity and positive match rate
**So that** I am rewarded for being a quality participant in the matching ecosystem

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I have been a member for more than 30 days and have a mutual-acceptance rate above 30%
  **When** my daily swipe limit is calculated
  **Then** I receive a higher quota than the default new-user limit

- **Given** I have earned an increased limit
  **When** I view the daily swipe counter
  **Then** the counter reflects my personalized quota, not the default

## Notes
Limit tiers should not be surfaced as a gamification scoreboard — just silently applied to avoid unhealthy competition.
