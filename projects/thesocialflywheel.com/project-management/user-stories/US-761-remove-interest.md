# US-761: Remove Interest

## User Story

**As a** cautious newcomer
**I want to** remove an interest I no longer want associated with my profile
**So that** channels and users related to that topic stop appearing in my feed.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I have at least one interest
  **When** I tap Remove next to it and confirm
  **Then** it is deleted from my profile and excluded from interest-based matching within 24 hours.

## Notes
Removal confirmation prevents accidental deletion; the dialog also offers a "Reduce weight instead" option.
