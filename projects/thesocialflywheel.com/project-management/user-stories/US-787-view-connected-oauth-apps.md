# US-787: View Connected OAuth Apps

## User Story

**As a** cautious newcomer
**I want to** see which third-party apps have been granted OAuth access to my Flywheel Social account
**So that** I can audit and control my data sharing

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I navigate to Account Settings > Connected Apps
  **When** the list loads
  **Then** each app shows its name, the permissions granted, and the date it was authorised.
