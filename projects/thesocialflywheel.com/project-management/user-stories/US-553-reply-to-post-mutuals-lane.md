# US-553: Reply to a Post in the Mutuals Lane

## User Story

**As a** Social Connector
**I want to** reply to posts in my Mutuals lane
**So that** I can start conversations with people I know

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am viewing a post in the Mutuals lane
  **When** I tap Reply and submit text
  **Then** my reply appears nested under the original post and is visible to others in the mutuals web

- **Given** I submit a reply
  **When** the post author is within my mutuals web
  **Then** they receive an in-app notification within 10 seconds

## Notes
Reply box should support inline media attachments up to the channel size limit.
