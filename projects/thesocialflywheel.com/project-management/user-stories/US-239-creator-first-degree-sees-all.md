# US-239: Creator First-Degree Sees All

## User Story

**As a** Creator (P-009)
**I want to** know that all of my 1st-degree mutuals will see every post I publish, regardless of its topic
**So that** I can create freely without worrying that my content will be filtered away from my closest audience

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I publish a post in any channel or with any interest tag
  **When** my 1st-degree mutuals' feeds are built
  **Then** the post appears in their Mutuals lane without interest filtering

- **Given** I view my post's reach analytics
  **When** analytics load
  **Then** I see a count labeled "Delivered to 1st-degree mutuals" that reflects full delivery, separate from outer-degree interest-filtered reach

## Notes
This gives Creators a predictable floor of guaranteed reach, incentivizing the mutual model over follower-only models.
