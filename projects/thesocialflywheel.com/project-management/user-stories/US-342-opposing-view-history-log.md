# US-342: Opposing-View History and Saved Posts Log

## User Story

**As a** debate seeker
**I want to** review a log of opposing-view posts I have previously seen or saved
**So that** I can track the breadth of perspectives I have encountered and revisit posts that were interesting

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I have seen opposing-view posts in my lane
  **When** I navigate to Profile > Opposing-Views History
  **Then** I see a chronological list of posts I was shown, with the triggering interest noted for each

- **Given** I saved a post
  **When** I view the history log
  **Then** saved posts are visually marked and I can filter to show only saved posts

## Notes
History retention window (e.g. 90 days) is a platform configuration. Posts removed by moderation action should be replaced by a placeholder "Post removed" entry.
