# US-265: See Who Expressed Interest in Me

## User Story

**As a** Social Connector (P-003)
**I want to** see a list of people who have swiped right on me
**So that** I can decide whether to accept, reject, or preview their posts before deciding

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** someone has swiped right on me
  **When** I open my Match Inbox
  **Then** their card appears with our shared interests highlighted and a preview of their recent posts

- **Given** I have not responded to an interest within 7 days
  **When** I view my inbox
  **Then** the card shows a "Expiring soon" badge and the remaining days
