# US-214: Feed Weighting Tooltip

## User Story

**As a** Bridge-Builder (P-001)
**I want to** access a tooltip or info panel that explains how degree weighting affects my feed order
**So that** I understand why certain posts appear at the top and can make informed decisions about expanding my web

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am viewing my main feed
  **When** I tap the "ⓘ Feed" icon in the feed header
  **Then** a tooltip or bottom sheet explains that 1st-degree posts rank highest, followed by 2nd–4th degree by interest match, with links to manage interests and mutuals

- **Given** the tooltip is displayed
  **When** I tap "Manage my mutuals"
  **Then** I am taken directly to the mutuals management screen
