# US-699: Public Transparency Report

## User Story

**As a** bridge-builder who cares about platform integrity
**I want to** read a regular public transparency report from the platform
**So that** I can assess whether the platform's moderation practices are fair, consistent, and proportionate

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** the platform has completed a quarter
  **When** the transparency report is published
  **Then** it is accessible at a stable public URL and includes: total reports received by category, percentage actioned vs dismissed, appeal volumes and overturn rates, global bans applied, and CSAM reports submitted to authorities

- **Given** I am reading the transparency report
  **When** I view the data
  **Then** all figures are broken down by quarter and compared to the previous quarter so I can see trends

- **Given** the transparency report is published
  **When** I access it
  **Then** no individual user, reporter, or channel is identified in any figure or example — all data is aggregated to protect privacy

## Notes
Publishing a transparency report on a quarterly cadence signals platform accountability and is increasingly expected by regulators and users alike.
