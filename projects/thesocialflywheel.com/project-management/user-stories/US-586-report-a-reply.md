# US-586: Report a Reply

## User Story

**As a** Channel Moderator
**I want to** report an abusive reply
**So that** the trust-and-safety team can review it promptly

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I tap the options menu on a reply
  **When** I select Report
  **Then** I see the standard report categories: harassment, spam, misinformation, other

- **Given** I submit the report
  **Then** I see a confirmation that the reply is under review; the reply remains visible to me until a decision is made

## Notes
Reporting a reply does not auto-hide it from the reporter's view. Moderator reports are weighted higher in the review queue.
