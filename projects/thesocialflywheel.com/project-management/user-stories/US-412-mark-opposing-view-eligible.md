# US-412: Mark Post as Opposing-View Eligible

## User Story

**As a** Bridge-Builder
**I want to** opt my post into the Opposing-Views discovery lane
**So that** users with differing perspectives can encounter and engage with my content

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am composing or editing a post
  **When** I toggle "Allow in Opposing-Views lane" to on
  **Then** the post is eligible for sampling into non-mutual users' Opposing-Views feeds

- **Given** my post appears in a non-mutual's Opposing-Views lane
  **When** they view it
  **Then** they can react or reply but cannot see my full profile unless we become mutuals

## Notes
Only posts tagged with at least one interest are eligible for the Opposing-Views lane.
