# US-587: Pin a Top Reply on My Post

## User Story

**As a** Creator
**I want to** pin a reply to the top of my post's thread
**So that** important responses are immediately visible to all readers

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I tap options on a reply to my own post
  **When** I select "Pin reply"
  **Then** the reply moves to the top of the thread with a "Pinned" label

- **Given** I pin a new reply
  **Then** the previously pinned reply unpins automatically; only one pinned reply is allowed per post

## Notes
Only the post author can pin or unpin replies. Pinned reply is still subject to normal reporting and moderation.
