# US-366: Discovery Items Visually Distinct in Feed

## User Story

**As a** Quiet Consumer
**I want to** distinguish discovery items from regular feed posts at a glance
**So that** I always know whether I am seeing content from my mutuals or discovery-surfaced content

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** a discovery item appears in the feed
  **When** I view the feed
  **Then** the discovery item card has a consistent visual treatment (e.g., a labeled badge or border) that differentiates it from standard posts

- **Given** I am using the app in high-contrast or dark mode
  **When** a discovery item appears
  **Then** the visual distinction remains legible without relying solely on low-contrast color differences

## Notes
Visual treatment must be established in the design system and applied consistently across web and mobile clients.
