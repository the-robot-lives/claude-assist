# US-596: Understand Engagement Rules in Swipe-to-Match Lane

## User Story

**As a** Cautious Newcomer
**I want to** understand what engagement is allowed in the Swipe-to-Match lane
**So that** I don't accidentally violate others' expectations or my own privacy

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I enter the Swipe-to-Match lane for the first time
  **When** I view an intro card
  **Then** a tooltip explains: "React to show interest — no replies until you match"

- **Given** I match with someone
  **When** the match is confirmed
  **Then** a direct reply thread opens between us and standard mutuals engagement unlocks for that relationship

## Notes
Swipe-to-Match reactions are private — only visible to the other party after a mutual match is confirmed.
