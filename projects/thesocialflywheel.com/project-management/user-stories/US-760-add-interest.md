# US-760: Add Interest

## User Story

**As a** niche enthusiast
**I want to** add interests to my profile
**So that** the algorithm surfaces content and channels relevant to my passions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I open Interest Management
  **When** I search for "competitive birdwatching" and tap Add
  **Then** it appears in my interests list and the Discovery lane updates within one session.

## Notes
Users may hold up to 50 interests simultaneously; attempting to add a 51st prompts removal of an existing one.
