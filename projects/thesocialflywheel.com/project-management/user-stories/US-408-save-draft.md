# US-408: Save a Post as a Draft

## User Story

**As a** Creator
**I want to** save my in-progress post as a draft
**So that** I can finish and publish it later without losing my work

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am composing a post
  **When** I tap "Save Draft"
  **Then** the post is saved to my Drafts list and the composer closes

- **Given** I have one or more drafts
  **When** I open the Drafts section
  **Then** I see all saved drafts ordered by last-modified date

## Notes
Drafts include all attached media, tags, and audience settings at time of save.
