# US-136: Pin And Feature Posts On Creator Profile

## User Story

**As a** creator
**I want to** pin or feature specific posts on my profile
**So that** new visitors see my best or most relevant work first

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am viewing one of my posts
  **When** I choose to pin it to my profile
  **Then** the post appears in a featured section at the top of my profile

- **Given** I have pinned posts
  **When** I pin an additional post beyond the allowed limit
  **Then** I am prompted to unpin one first, keeping the featured set bounded

## Notes
Suggest a small fixed cap (e.g. 3 featured posts) to keep profiles scannable.
