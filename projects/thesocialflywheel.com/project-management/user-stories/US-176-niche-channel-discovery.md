# US-176: Discover Niche Low-Population Channels

## User Story

**As a** Niche Enthusiast
**I want to** discover small, specialized channels that are highly relevant to my specific interests even if they have few members
**So that** I can find my people in niche communities rather than being funneled only into large popular channels

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am on the Discover tab
  **When** I select the "Niche" filter or "Small Communities" section
  **Then** I see channels with fewer than 100 members that have high tag overlap with my interest profile, ranked by tag relevance over member count

- **Given** a suggested channel has fewer than 25 members
  **When** it is displayed in the directory or suggestions
  **Then** it shows a "Small community" label instead of a raw member count, to reduce stigma from low numbers

## Notes
Niche discovery should not be limited by activity recency — a quiet channel with high interest alignment is still valuable to a niche enthusiast. Platform should not hide channels simply because they are small.
