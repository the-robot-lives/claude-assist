# US-347: Feedback Survey on Opposing-View Experience

## User Story

**As a** bridge-builder
**I want to** be periodically prompted to share brief feedback on my Opposing-Views Lane experience
**So that** the product team can learn whether the lane is meeting its goal of respectful perspective exposure

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I have used the Opposing-Views Lane for at least 14 days
  **When** the feedback prompt triggers
  **Then** a non-blocking banner asks "Is the Opposing-Views Lane working for you?" with thumbs up/down and an optional comment field

- **Given** I respond to or dismiss the prompt
  **When** the next prompt window opens
  **Then** the prompt does not reappear for at least 60 days

## Notes
Feedback prompts must be dismissible without answering and must never block access to the feed. Response data is aggregated and anonymized before product team review.
