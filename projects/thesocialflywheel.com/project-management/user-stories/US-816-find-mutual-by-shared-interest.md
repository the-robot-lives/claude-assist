# US-816: Find a Mutual by Shared Interest

## User Story

**As a** social connector
**I want to** search for people who share a specific interest and are within my mutual network
**So that** I can discover moots who might become close connections

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I search for an interest topic and filter to People
  **When** results appear
  **Then** I can further toggle "Mutuals only" to see only 1st-degree moots who follow or engage with that interest

- **Given** mutual matches are shown
  **When** I hover or focus a card
  **Then** the shared interests between me and that person are highlighted

## Notes
"Mutuals only" toggle is additive on top of any active degree filter.
