# US-791: Pause Swipe Lane

## User Story

**As a** cautious newcomer
**I want to** temporarily pause the Swipe-to-Match lane
**So that** I am not discoverable to potential matches while I take a break from meeting new people

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I open Settings > Swipe & Match
  **When** I toggle "Pause Swipe Lane" on
  **Then** I no longer appear in other users' swipe queues and the lane is hidden from my own feed until I toggle it off.
