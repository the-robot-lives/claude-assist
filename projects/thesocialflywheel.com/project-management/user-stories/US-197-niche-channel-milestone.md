# US-197: Niche Channel Growth Milestone Celebration

## User Story

**As a** Niche Enthusiast who joined a small channel early
**I want to** receive a celebratory notification and Founding Member badge when the channel reaches key membership milestones
**So that** I feel recognized for being an early supporter and am motivated to stay engaged with niche communities

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I joined a channel when it had fewer than 25 members
  **When** the channel crosses the 25-member threshold
  **Then** I receive an in-app notification celebrating the milestone and a permanent "Founding Member" badge is added to my membership for that channel

- **Given** a channel I belong to hits the 100-member milestone
  **When** the milestone is crossed
  **Then** all members receive a channel-wide announcement post celebrating the growth (posted by the system, visible in the Mutuals lane)

## Notes
Milestone thresholds: 25, 100, 500, 1000 members. Founding Member eligibility is frozen at the 25-member threshold — joining after that does not qualify even for later milestones.
