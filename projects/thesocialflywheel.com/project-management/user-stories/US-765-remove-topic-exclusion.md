# US-765: Remove Topic Exclusion

## User Story

**As a** bridge-builder
**I want to** remove a topic exclusion I previously set
**So that** I can re-engage with a subject now that I am ready.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I have one or more topic exclusions
  **When** I tap Remove on an exclusion and confirm
  **Then** that topic's posts are no longer filtered and begin appearing in relevant lanes within the current session.

## Notes
A brief re-introduction notice appears after removing an exclusion to orient the user to newly visible content.
