# US-017: Tutorial — Opposing Views Lane Explanation

## User Story

**As a** bridge-builder
**I want to** the onboarding tutorial to explain the Opposing Views lane
**So that** new users understand it is read-only and why it exists

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am in the Opposing Views lane tutorial slide
  **When** I read it
  **Then** the copy clearly states: (1) this lane is read-only, (2) content is from people with different perspectives on a shared interest, (3) the ratio is fixed to avoid echo-chamber reinforcement.

- **Given** I try to post while in the Opposing Views lane
  **When** I tap the compose button
  **Then** I see a contextual message explaining posts must be made in the Mutuals lane.

## Notes
Tone should be neutral and aspirational — avoid framing "opposing" negatively. Link to a fuller help article from the tutorial slide.
