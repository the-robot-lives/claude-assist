# US-778: Opt In to Email Digest

## User Story

**As a** bridge-builder
**I want to** opt into a periodic email digest of activity in my network
**So that** I can review highlights without opening the app every day

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I open Notification Settings
  **When** I enable "Weekly Email Digest"
  **Then** I receive a summary email every Monday listing top posts from my Mutuals lane and any new match suggestions.
