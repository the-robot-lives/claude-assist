# US-753: Change Handle

## User Story

**As a** creator
**I want to** change my @handle
**So that** my username reflects my current brand identity.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am on Account Settings
  **When** I enter a new handle that is available and meets format rules
  **Then** my handle is updated and old handle references in mentions are redirected for 30 days.

## Notes
Handles must be 3–30 alphanumeric/underscore characters. Users may change handle once every 30 days.
