# US-556: Repost a Post to My Mutuals Web

## User Story

**As a** Social Connector
**I want to** repost a piece of content to my mutuals web
**So that** I can share interesting finds with people I trust

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am viewing a post in the Mutuals lane
  **When** I tap Repost and confirm
  **Then** the post appears in my mutual followers' feeds attributed to the original author

- **Given** the repost is live
  **When** the original author is within my web
  **Then** they are notified that I reposted their content

## Notes
Repost inherits the original post's lane visibility rules. Plain repost carries no added commentary (see US-557 for quote-post).
