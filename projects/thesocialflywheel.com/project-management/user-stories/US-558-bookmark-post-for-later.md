# US-558: Bookmark a Post for Later

## User Story

**As a** Quiet Consumer
**I want to** bookmark posts
**So that** I can revisit them later without the author being notified

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am viewing any post
  **When** I tap the bookmark icon
  **Then** the post is added to my private Saved list and the icon fills to confirm the action

- **Given** I open my profile's Saved list
  **Then** I see all bookmarked posts in reverse chronological order with a timestamp

## Notes
Bookmarks are private; no notification is sent to the original author. Available in all lanes including Opposing-Views.
