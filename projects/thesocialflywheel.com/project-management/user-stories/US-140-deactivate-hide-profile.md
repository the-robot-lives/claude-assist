# US-140: Temporarily Deactivate Or Hide Profile

## User Story

**As a** cautious newcomer
**I want to** temporarily deactivate or hide my profile
**So that** I can step away without permanently deleting my account or losing my mutuals

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I deactivate my profile
  **When** other users search for or browse to me
  **Then** my profile is hidden from discovery, search, and lanes while my data and mutuals are preserved

- **Given** my profile is deactivated
  **When** I log back in and reactivate
  **Then** my profile, interests, and mutual relationships are restored intact

## Notes
Distinguish clearly from account deletion; reassure cautious users that deactivation is fully reversible.
