# US-025: Onboarding Checklist Completion Milestone

## User Story

**As a** new user
**I want to** a congratulatory moment when I complete all onboarding checklist items
**So that** I feel motivated and ready to engage more deeply with the community

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** all checklist items are marked complete
  **When** the last item is ticked
  **Then** a celebratory animation plays and a full-screen "You're all set!" card appears with suggested channels to explore.

- **Given** I dismiss the completion card
  **When** I return to the home screen
  **Then** the checklist widget is gone and a subtle "Profile complete" badge appears on my avatar.

## Notes
Keep animation short (< 3 s) and skippable. Respects system "reduce motion" preference.
