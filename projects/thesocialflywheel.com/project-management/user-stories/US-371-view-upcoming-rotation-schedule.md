# US-371: View Upcoming Rotation Schedule

## User Story

**As a** Niche Enthusiast
**I want to** see a preview of which adjacent topic clusters are scheduled for the next rotation
**So that** I can anticipate upcoming discovery themes and decide whether to engage or adjust preferences

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I navigate to the Discovery Settings page
  **When** I tap "Upcoming Rotation"
  **Then** I see a list of topic clusters slated for the next monthly period with their scheduled start date

- **Given** I am viewing the upcoming rotation schedule
  **When** I tap a topic in the list
  **Then** I can immediately mark it as excluded before it becomes active

## Notes
Only show the immediate next rotation cycle; multi-month ahead visibility is out of scope.
