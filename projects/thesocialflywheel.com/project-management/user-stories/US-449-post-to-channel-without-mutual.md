# US-449: Post to a Channel Without Requiring a Mutual Connection

## User Story

**As a** Niche Enthusiast
**I want to** post directly to an interest channel even if I have few or no mutuals there
**So that** I can contribute to niche communities and build my presence without needing pre-existing connections

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am a member of an interest channel
  **When** I compose a post and select that channel as the destination
  **Then** the post appears in the channel feed regardless of how many mutuals I have in that channel

- **Given** a non-mutual sees my channel post
  **When** they view it
  **Then** they can react, reply, or initiate a mutual request; I receive notifications for all three

## Notes
Channel membership (subscription) is required to post; open channels allow anyone to join.
