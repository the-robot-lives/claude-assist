# US-956: Track Mutuals-Web Growth Over Time

## User Story

**As a** Social Connector
**I want to** see a chart of how many new mutuals I added each week and month
**So that** I can measure the health and growth rate of my social network.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** an account with mutual history
  **When** I open the Mutuals Growth chart
  **Then** I see a bar chart of net new mutuals per week for the past 12 weeks.

- **Given** the Mutuals Growth chart
  **When** I switch to monthly view
  **Then** I see monthly net-new mutuals for the past 12 months.

## Notes
"Net new" = added minus removed. Chart supports toggling between weekly and monthly granularity.
