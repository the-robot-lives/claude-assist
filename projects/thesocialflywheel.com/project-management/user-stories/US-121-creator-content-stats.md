# US-121: Display Creator Content And Post Stats On Profile

## User Story

**As a** creator
**I want to** show content and post statistics on my profile
**So that** visitors can see my activity and contributions at a glance

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I have published posts and content
  **When** a viewer opens my profile
  **Then** they see aggregate stats such as post count, channels I contribute to, and recent activity

- **Given** I want to control what is shown
  **When** I open profile settings
  **Then** I can toggle which stat categories are visible

## Notes
Stats should respect lane visibility so non-mutuals only see what their degree allows.
