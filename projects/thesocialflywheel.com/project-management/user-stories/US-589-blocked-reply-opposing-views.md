# US-589: See Blocked Reply Control in Opposing-Views Lane

## User Story

**As a** Debate Seeker
**I want to** clearly see that replying is not allowed in the Opposing-Views lane
**So that** I am not confused by the missing reply control

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I open a post in the Opposing-Views lane
  **When** I look for a reply button
  **Then** it is absent and replaced with a "This lane is read-only" info label

- **Given** I want to share my thoughts on the topic
  **When** I tap the info label
  **Then** I am offered a shortcut to start a new post in my Mutuals lane referencing the same topic

## Notes
Ensures the Opposing-Views lane remains pressure-free for authors while giving readers a constructive outlet.
