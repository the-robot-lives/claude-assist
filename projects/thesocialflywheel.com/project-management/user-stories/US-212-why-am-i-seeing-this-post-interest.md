# US-212: Why Am I Seeing This Post — Interest

## User Story

**As a** Niche Enthusiast (P-002)
**I want to** see which of my interests caused a 2nd–4th degree post to appear in my feed
**So that** I can tune my interest subscriptions to control which outer-degree content reaches me

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** a post from a 3rd-degree mutual appears because of an interest overlap
  **When** I open "Why am I seeing this?"
  **Then** the explanation lists the matched interest(s) (e.g., "This post matches your interest in #Ceramics")

- **Given** a post matches multiple interests
  **When** the explanation is shown
  **Then** all matching interests are listed and I can tap any one to manage that interest subscription inline
