# US-764: Add Topic Exclusion

## User Story

**As a** cautious newcomer
**I want to** permanently exclude specific topics or belief categories from all my lanes
**So that** I never encounter content on those subjects regardless of source.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I open Exclusions in settings
  **When** I add a topic (e.g. "political violence") and save
  **Then** posts tagged with or algorithmically matching that topic are hidden across all lanes with no exceptions.

## Notes
Exclusions differ from muted keywords: they apply to topic-level categorisation, not just surface text matching.
