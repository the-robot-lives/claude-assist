# US-409: Schedule a Post for Future Publication

## User Story

**As a** Creator
**I want to** set a future date and time for my post to publish automatically
**So that** I can plan content in advance and reach my audience at optimal times

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am composing a post
  **When** I tap "Schedule" and pick a date/time at least 5 minutes in the future
  **Then** the post is saved as a scheduled draft and appears in my Scheduled list

- **Given** a scheduled post exists
  **When** the scheduled time arrives
  **Then** the post is published automatically with all original settings intact

## Notes
Scheduled posts can be edited or cancelled before the publish time.
