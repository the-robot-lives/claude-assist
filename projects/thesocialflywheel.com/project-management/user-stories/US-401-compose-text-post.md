# US-401: Compose a Text Post

## User Story

**As a** Creator
**I want to** compose and publish a plain-text post
**So that** my thoughts reach my mutuals and relevant interest channels immediately

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am logged in and on the home feed
  **When** I tap the compose button and type text, then tap Publish
  **Then** the post appears in my mutuals' Mutuals lane within seconds

- **Given** I have typed a post
  **When** I tap Publish with no internet connection
  **Then** the post is queued and published automatically when connectivity resumes

## Notes
Character limit indicator must be visible while composing (see US-443).
