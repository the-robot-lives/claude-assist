# US-361: Discover New Channels via Adjacent Interest

## User Story

**As a** Niche Enthusiast
**I want to** be shown channels that are topically adjacent to my current interests
**So that** I can find focused communities I did not know existed

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** I am active in the Topology channel under my Mathematics interest
  **When** the discovery engine is computing adjacent content
  **Then** it surfaces channel cards for related channels such as Analysis or Geometry that share mutual member overlap

- **Given** a suggested channel card appears in my feed
  **When** I tap to preview the channel
  **Then** I can see recent posts and join without leaving the discovery feed flow

## Notes
Channel discovery cards are clearly distinguished from post-level discovery items visually.
