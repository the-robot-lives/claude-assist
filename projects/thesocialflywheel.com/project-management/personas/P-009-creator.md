# P-009: Tomás Vidal

| Attribute | Value |

## Bio
Tomás creates and shares work to grow an audience. He cares about reach, how the mutuals graph propagates his posts outward, and tools that help him understand what resonates — all without the platform forcing him to chase virality.

## Goals
- Reach an audience that genuinely cares, propagated through the mutuals web.
- Understand how his posts travel across degrees and interests.

## Frustrations
- Reach throttled unless he pays or games the algorithm.
- No insight into how content spreads.

## Behaviors
- Posts regularly with rich media.
- Reviews personal analytics on reach and engagement.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I share my work, I want it to travel through people who care, so I can build a real audience without gaming a black box."

## Relationship to Product
Primary content producer; his output and retention drive channel vitality.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Reach insight — Tomás sees how a post spread to 3rd-degree mutuals via a shared interest.
- **Scenario 2:** Media post — Tomás publishes an image carousel and tags relevant interests.
