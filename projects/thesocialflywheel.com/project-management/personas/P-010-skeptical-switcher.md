# P-010: Grace Kim

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 52 |
| Occupation | Small-business owner |
| Location | rural Idaho |
| Tech comfort | low |

## Bio
Grace is trying Flywheel on an older Android phone over a spotty rural connection. She's skeptical of social media in general and will leave at the first sign of confusion, slowness, or manipulation. She represents the low-bandwidth, low-tech-comfort edge of the audience.

## Goals
- Use the core features reliably on a slow connection and modest hardware.
- Trust that the product isn't manipulating or harvesting her.

## Frustrations
- Heavy, slow apps that drain data and battery.
- Dark patterns and confusing flows.

## Behaviors
- Uses only core features; avoids advanced settings.
- Quick to abandon if onboarding is confusing.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I try a new app on my old phone, I want it to be fast, clear, and honest, so I can decide quickly whether it's worth my time."

## Relationship to Product
Churn-risk edge user; performance budget and clarity determine whether she stays.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Lean load — Flywheel loads her feed quickly on 3G with reduced media.
- **Scenario 2:** Plain onboarding — Grace completes signup without hitting jargon or dark patterns.
