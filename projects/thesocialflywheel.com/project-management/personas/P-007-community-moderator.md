# P-007: Marcus Bell

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 34 |
| Occupation | IT manager / volunteer moderator |
| Location | Toronto, CA |
| Tech comfort | high |

## Bio
Marcus runs several interest channels and cares deeply about keeping them healthy. He needs efficient moderation tooling, clear reporting queues, and visibility into how the mutuals graph and exclusions interact with the content people see in his channels.

## Goals
- Keep his channels welcoming and on-topic with minimal manual toil.
- Act quickly and fairly on reports and rule violations.

## Frustrations
- Moderation tools that are slow, blunt, or lack context.
- No insight into why problematic content reached users.

## Behaviors
- Triages report queues daily.
- Configures channel rules, auto-moderation thresholds, and appeals.

## Job to Be Done
> "When activity spikes in my channel, I want fast, contextual moderation tools, so I can keep it healthy without burning out."

## Relationship to Product
Tertiary power user whose tooling needs differ sharply from end users; critical to community health.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Report triage — Marcus works a queue of reported posts with one-click actions.
- **Scenario 2:** Appeal review — Marcus reviews a user's appeal of a removed post.
