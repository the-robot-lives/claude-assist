# P-001: Maya Chen

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 31 |
| Occupation | UX researcher |
| Location | Portland, OR |
| Tech comfort | high |

## Bio
Maya joined Flywheel because every other feed felt like an echo chamber. She genuinely enjoys discovering that someone she admires on one topic disagrees with her on another — she finds it humanizing rather than threatening. She is the archetypal user the product is designed around.

## Goals
- Stay close to the interests and people she loves while being exposed to new and opposing ideas in a manageable, respectful dose.
- Build a mutuals web of people she actually trusts, not a follower count.

## Frustrations
- Algorithmic feeds that narrow over time until everything looks the same.
- Hostile comment sections where disagreement turns into pile-ons.

## Behaviors
- Reads opposing-view posts thoughtfully before forming an opinion.
- Curates her interests deliberately and tunes discovery rotation.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I open a channel about something I love, I want to see both kindred spirits and respectful dissent, so I can grow without being overwhelmed."

## Relationship to Product
Daily active user; treats Flywheel as her primary social network and the place she goes to think out loud.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Opposing serendipity — A frisbee mutual posts about hating sushi (which Maya loves); she engages with the opposing-view lane for the first time.
- **Scenario 2:** Web expansion — She accepts a swipe match and watches her reachable degrees widen.
