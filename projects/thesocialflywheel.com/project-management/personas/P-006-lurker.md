# P-006: Lena Hoffmann

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 45 |
| Occupation | Accountant |
| Location | Munich, DE |
| Tech comfort | medium |

## Bio
Lena rarely posts but reads constantly. She wants a calm, high-signal feed of things she's interested in and occasional new ideas — without pressure to perform or participate. The bulk of Flywheel's audience behaves like her.

## Goals
- Enjoy a relevant, low-noise feed without having to post.
- Occasionally discover something new without being overwhelmed.

## Frustrations
- Feeds that punish non-posters with worse content.
- Pressure to engage publicly.

## Behaviors
- Scrolls daily, reacts occasionally, rarely posts.
- Relies on discovery rotation for novelty.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I have a few quiet minutes, I want a relevant and lightly surprising feed, so I can enjoy reading without any obligation to participate."

## Relationship to Product
The silent majority; success depends on serving her well even though she produces little content.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Calm scroll — Lena reads her mutuals' posts and a few discovery items on her commute.
- **Scenario 2:** Gentle nudge — A rotating discovery topic catches her interest and she follows it.
