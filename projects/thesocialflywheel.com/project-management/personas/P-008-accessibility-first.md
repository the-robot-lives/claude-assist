# P-008: Aisha Nasser

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 27 |
| Occupation | Disability advocate & writer |
| Location | Cairo, EG |
| Tech comfort | high |

## Bio
Aisha is blind and navigates entirely by screen reader and keyboard. She is an experienced power user of assistive technology and abandons products that treat accessibility as an afterthought. Her needs surface design flaws that hurt everyone.

## Goals
- Navigate channels, swipe-match, and the feed entirely by keyboard and screen reader.
- Understand the social-graph and visibility concepts through clear non-visual cues.

## Frustrations
- Unlabeled controls and focus traps.
- Visual-only affordances (e.g., degree weighting shown only by subtle styling).

## Behaviors
- Relies on landmarks, headings, and ARIA live regions.
- Uses high-contrast and reduced-motion settings.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I use any feature, I want full parity through my screen reader, so I can participate equally without workarounds."

## Relationship to Product
Edge-case user whose requirements set the accessibility bar (WCAG 2.2 AA minimum).

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Keyboard swipe — Aisha completes a swipe-match session entirely by keyboard.
- **Scenario 2:** Announced context — A live region announces why a post appears (degree/interest).
