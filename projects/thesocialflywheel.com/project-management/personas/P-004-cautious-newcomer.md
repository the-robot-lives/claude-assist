# P-004: Sam Whitfield

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 38 |
| Occupation | Nurse |
| Location | Manchester, UK |
| Tech comfort | medium |

## Bio
Sam was burned by oversharing on an old social network and is wary of any new one. They want firm control over who sees their posts and reassurance that exposure to opposing views won't mean exposure to harassment. Strong, legible privacy controls are the price of admission.

## Goals
- Understand and control exactly who can see their content before posting anything.
- Exclude specific topics and beliefs from ever reaching them.

## Frustrations
- Confusing, buried privacy settings.
- Discovering after the fact that strangers could see their posts.

## Behaviors
- Reads privacy explanations carefully during onboarding.
- Sets up exclusions and block-cascade preferences early.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I join a new network, I want clear control over my visibility and exclusions, so I can participate without feeling exposed."

## Relationship to Product
Cautious adopter; converts to regular use only once trust is established.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Exclusion setup — During onboarding, Sam excludes a belief category from discovery and opposing-views lanes.
- **Scenario 2:** Visibility preview — Before a first post, Sam checks exactly which degrees will see it.
