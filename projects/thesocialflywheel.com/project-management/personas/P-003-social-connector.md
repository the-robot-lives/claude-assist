## Bio
Priya is a natural connector who thrives on meeting new people and introducing them to each other. The mutuals web is her favorite mechanic — she loves watching her reachable network expand with every accepted match and seeing degrees-of-separation play out.

## Goals
- Grow a rich, high-quality mutuals web quickly.
- Understand who is connected to whom and how content reaches her.

## Frustrations
- Vanity follower counts that don't reflect real relationships.
- Not understanding why she sees a given post.

## Behaviors
- Swipes actively and accepts matches often.
- Checks degree-of-separation and "why am I seeing this" explanations.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I find someone interesting, I want to connect and watch my world expand, so my feed keeps getting richer through people I chose."

## Relationship to Product
High-frequency user driving the growth flywheel; her acceptances widen many other users' graphs.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Match streak — She swipes through a batch of subject-matter matches in one sitting.
- **Scenario 2:** Degree weighting — She notices a 2nd-degree mutual's post ranked above a 4th-degree one.
