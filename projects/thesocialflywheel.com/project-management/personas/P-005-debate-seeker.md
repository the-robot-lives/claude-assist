# P-005: Jordan Alvarez

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 29 |
| Occupation | Policy analyst |
| Location | Washington, DC |
| Tech comfort | high |

## Bio
Jordan actively seeks out viewpoints unlike their own and finds most platforms too comfortable. The opposing-views lane is the feature that drew them to Flywheel — they want to understand how people who disagree with them actually think, within shared interests.

## Goals
- Maximize exposure to thoughtful opposing viewpoints on topics they care about.
- Understand the reasoning of people unlike them without descending into flame wars.

## Frustrations
- Echo chambers that only confirm existing beliefs.
- Opposing views that arrive as bad-faith trolling rather than genuine difference.

## Behaviors
- Adjusts the opposing-views ratio higher than default.
- Reads (and respects the read-only constraint of) the opposing-views lane heavily.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I follow a topic, I want a steady, respectful stream of people who see it differently, so I can sharpen my own thinking."

## Relationship to Product
Engaged power user who stress-tests the opposing-views and exclusion systems.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Ratio tuning — Jordan raises the opposing-view ratio for the politics channel.
- **Scenario 2:** Read-only boundary — Jordan wants to respond to an opposing post and learns the lane is intentionally one-way.
