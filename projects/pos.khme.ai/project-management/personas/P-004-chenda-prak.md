# Chenda Prak — Multi-Store Operator

## Demographics

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Age** | 35-50 |
| **Role** | Owner/operator, 3-5 minimart or retail locations |
| **Technical Level** | Intermediate-to-advanced |
| **Industry** | Multi-location convenience/general retail |
| **Location** | Phnom Penh with locations spanning city + 1-2 provincial towns |

## Bio

Chenda expanded from a single successful minimart into a small chain over six years, and now spends most of her time managing managers rather than standing behind a register herself. She juggles separate spreadsheets and messages from each store manager to piece together a picture of the business, which she knows is unsustainable as she considers opening a sixth location. She thinks in terms of consolidated numbers, not individual transactions.

## Goals
1. See consolidated sales, stock, and cash performance across all stores from one dashboard.
2. Set permission tiers so store managers can manage their own store without seeing or touching others.
3. Spot underperforming or high-shrinkage locations early using cross-store comparison and forecasting.
4. Maintain an audit trail that lets her investigate any location's activity without visiting in person.

## Frustrations
1. Store-level numbers arrive late, inconsistently formatted, or not at all from some managers.
2. No single source of truth — she reconciles Telegram messages, photos of paper ledgers, and spreadsheets.
3. Can't easily compare which store's inventory turns are healthy versus which are bleeding cash.
4. Worries that trusted managers at distant locations have blind spots she can't see remotely.

## Behaviors
- Reviews numbers primarily from her phone or laptop, rarely from a store's physical register.
- Holds weekly calls with store managers to compile numbers manually today.
- Delegates day-to-day operations fully but wants alerting on anomalies (shrinkage spikes, stockouts).
- Willing to pay for a premium/paid tier if it genuinely saves her reconciliation time.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I need to check on all my stores at once, I want one consolidated view with drill-down per location, so I can spot problems early without depending on managers to report them to me."

## Relationship to Product
Chenda is the anchor for the paid multi-store tier — she discovers the product either by a store manager (a Dara Lim-type owner who scaled up) recommending it, or through targeted outreach once the vendor identifies multi-location accounts. Her adoption depends entirely on the consolidated reporting and role-based permissions working correctly across locations; if single-store owners like Dara are the entry point, Chenda represents the expansion revenue and the feature set that justifies a paid subscription over the free single-store tier. Forecasting and cross-store audit trail are her signature features — she has little use for the single-store daily sell screen herself. Churn risk: data lag or inconsistency between store-level and consolidated views, or a permission model that lets a manager see another store's numbers by mistake.

## Scenarios
1. **Weekly cross-store review** — Chenda opens a consolidated dashboard comparing sales, shrinkage rate, and stock turns across her 5 stores to decide where to invest in additional stock or address a problem.
2. **Anomaly investigation** — An alert flags an unusual cash discrepancy at one location; Chenda drills into that store's audit trail remotely to see exactly which staff member and shift it happened under, without traveling there.
