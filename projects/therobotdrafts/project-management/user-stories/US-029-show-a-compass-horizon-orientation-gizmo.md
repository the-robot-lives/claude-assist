# US-029 — Show a compass/horizon orientation gizmo

**As** Marcus, the newly-onboarding engineer,
**I want** have a small compass or horizon gizmo always visible,
**so that** I always know which way is 'down' and 'home' while flying.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A persistent on-screen gizmo shows camera orientation relative to the model's axes
- [ ] Clicking a gizmo axis snaps to that orthographic-ish view
- [ ] A subtle horizon/grid plane reinforces 'down' without cluttering the model
- [ ] The gizmo stays readable against busy multi-hue backgrounds (contrast halo)

## Notes
Implements UX-review B8; orientation aids for a spatial learner (Marcus bio).
