# US-087 — Step through a pattern's collaboration sequence

**As** Elena, the CS educator,
**I want** step through a design pattern's runtime collaboration as a sequence highlight,
**so that** students see how the objects actually interact, not just the static classes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A region can be projected to a UML sequence diagram derived from the call structure
- [ ] I can step through the messages one at a time with the active message highlighted
- [ ] Stepping is bounded and projector-legible
- [ ] A region with no recoverable interaction explains that rather than showing an empty sequence

## Notes
Extends teaching to dynamic notations; builds on diagram coverage for Elena's lectures.
