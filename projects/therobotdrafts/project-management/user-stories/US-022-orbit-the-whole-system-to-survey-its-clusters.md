# US-022 — Orbit the whole system to survey its clusters

**As** Marcus, the newly-onboarding engineer,
**I want** slowly orbit the whole imported system to take in its major clusters,
**so that** I can build a first-day mental map before touching any code.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] An orbit control rotates the framed model around its center at a controllable speed
- [ ] Major clusters stay distinguishable and labeled throughout the orbit
- [ ] Orbit speed is bounded so it never becomes a disorienting whip-pan
- [ ] Orbit recenters on the framed model, not on empty space, so I never orbit around nothing

## Notes
Serves Marcus's first-day-map scenario (P-002 scenario 1); he learns spatially (P-002 bio).
