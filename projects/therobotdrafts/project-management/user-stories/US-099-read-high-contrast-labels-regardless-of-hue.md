# US-099 — Read high-contrast labels regardless of hue

**As** Theo, the accessibility-constrained developer,
**I want** read labels at high contrast and adjustable size independent of node hue,
**so that** I can read every distinction reliably regardless of color.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Labels render with a contrast halo and meet a high-contrast ratio against any kind hue background
- [ ] Label size is adjustable and the choice persists
- [ ] A high-contrast theme is available for the whole UI, not just node labels
- [ ] Labels stay legible at the zoomed-out LOD rather than degrading to unreadable pinpricks

## Notes
Serves Theo goal 3 (read labels reliably regardless of hue) and low-vision needs.
