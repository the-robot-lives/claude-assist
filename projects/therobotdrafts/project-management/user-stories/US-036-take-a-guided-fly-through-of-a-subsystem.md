# US-036 — Take a guided fly-through of a subsystem

**As** Marcus, the newly-onboarding engineer,
**I want** play a guided fly-through that walks me through a subsystem's main parts,
**so that** I get an orientation tour of the area I'm assigned to without a senior engineer's time.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A guided tour visits a curated sequence of nodes with bounded, comfortable camera moves
- [ ] Each stop shows a short caption explaining the element's role
- [ ] I can pause, step forward/back, and exit the tour at any point to free-explore
- [ ] Exiting mid-tour leaves me focused on the current stop, not snapped back to start

## Notes
Consumes Priya's shareable tours (US-041) from the learner side; supports Marcus goal 1.
