# US-041 — Record a guided fly-through tour to replay

**As** Priya, the tech lead,
**I want** record a guided fly-through of the system's subsystems that others can replay,
**so that** I can give a reusable onboarding walkthrough without repeating myself live.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Recording captures an ordered sequence of focus stops with optional captions
- [ ] A saved tour can be replayed by another user with comfortable, bounded camera moves
- [ ] Viewers can pause and free-explore, then resume the tour
- [ ] A tour stop pointing at a now-missing element is skipped with a note rather than flying to nothing
