# US-047 — Show a before/after of a proposed refactor

**As** Priya, the tech lead,
**I want** show a side-by-side before/after of a proposed structural change,
**so that** I can make the case for a refactor visually convincing.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A before/after view places the current and proposed structures side by side or as a toggle
- [ ] Differences (moved/added/removed) are marked consistently in both
- [ ] The comparison is shareable and presentable in presentation mode
- [ ] If the proposed change is empty the view says 'no change proposed' rather than showing two identical panels unlabeled

## Notes
Serves Priya's communicate-why goal; builds on diff (US-014) and impact preview (US-044).
