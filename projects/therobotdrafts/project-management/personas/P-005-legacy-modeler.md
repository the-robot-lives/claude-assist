# Robert Fields — Enterprise UML Modeler

## Demographics

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Age** | 45-58 |
| **Role** | Enterprise Architect / Modeling Lead |
| **Technical Level** | Advanced |
| **Industry** | Defense / government contracting |
| **Location** | On-site, Washington DC |

## Bio

Robert has modeled systems in Rational Rose and Sparx Enterprise Architect for two decades. He has a repository full of XMI and Rose petal files that represent years of institutional knowledge, and a process that demands full UML 2.5.1, SysML, and traceable interchange. He's skeptical of shiny tools that can't read his existing models.

## Goals

1. Bring existing Rose/EA/XMI models into a modern, navigable environment without losing fidelity
2. Produce standards-compliant diagrams across the full UML/SysML/BPMN/ArchiMate families
3. Round-trip models out again to the formats his organization mandates

## Frustrations

1. New tools support a toy subset of UML and break on real models
2. Interchange (XMI) import/export silently drops detail
3. Migration means re-modeling from scratch — a non-starter

## Behaviors

- Curates a versioned repository of model files
- Validates diagrams against notation standards meticulously
- Distrusts auto-layout that moves his carefully placed elements

## Job to Be Done

> "When I evaluate a new modeling environment, I want it to import my existing XMI/Rose/EA models with full fidelity and export them back, so I can adopt it without abandoning decades of work."

## Relationship to Product

Robert is the interchange and diagram-coverage user. He values full UML 2.5.1 coverage, XMI/Rose/EA import-export, and round-trip fidelity. He churns instantly if interchange is lossy or coverage is partial.

## Scenarios

1. **Migration trial** — Robert imports a legacy XMI package, checks that every relationship survived, and exports it back to compare.
2. **Notation review** — He projects a region to a SysML block diagram and verifies the notation matches the standard.
