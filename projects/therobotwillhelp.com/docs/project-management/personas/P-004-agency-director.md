## Bio
Lena manages client retainers where each account has recurring content and reporting work. She packages processes but must adapt quickly per client.

## Goals
- Reuse templates across clients while retaining per-client tuning.
- Keep quality checks and approvals visible to account leads.
- Track billable outcomes and output history per team member.

## Frustrations
- Too much custom setup per client.
- Hard to keep reusable quality standards consistent.
- Fear of silent failure when external context changes.

## Behaviors
- Uses templates extensively.
- Delegates approvals to senior operators.
- Requires client-safe exports and history logs.

## Job to Be Done
> When I run recurring client workflows, I want reusable robot teams and per-client settings, so I can deliver faster with consistent quality.

## Relationship to Product
Represents the packaging and template demand; strong fit for paid launch narratives.

## Scenarios
- **Client template launch:** She copies a support triage robot template and swaps policy docs in minutes.
- **Service audit:** She exports review decisions and rationale for quarterly client reporting.
