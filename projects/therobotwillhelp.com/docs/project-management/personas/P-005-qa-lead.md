# P-005: Sam Rivera

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 38 |
| Occupation | QA Lead, SaaS product team |
| Location | Austin, TX |
| Tech comfort | high |

## Bio
Sam is responsible for preventing avoidable quality slips. He is the final human checkpoint for customer-facing workflows.

## Goals
- Reduce bad output risk without adding manual overhead.
- Compare new robot behavior versions before rollout.
- Use concise evidence for rejections and revisions.

## Frustrations
- Binary review flows that hide why the model chose an answer.
- Weak confidence signals and weak source traceability.
- Too many context-heavy review screens.

## Behaviors
- Marks outputs with outcomes and risk levels.
- Prefers structured evidence over prose-only explanations.
- Builds checklists and acceptance criteria before launch.

## Job to Be Done
> When reviewing robot outputs, I want clear evidence and change history, so I can prevent quality regressions safely and quickly.

## Relationship to Product
Key validator of launch readiness and review controls.

## Scenarios
- **Post-launch review:** Sam compares the new QA robot version with previous output and decides on rollout.
- **Batch queue:** He batches 30 outputs and tags only risky outputs for escalation.
