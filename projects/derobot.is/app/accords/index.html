The Copacetic Accord

Verifiable commitments for AI agents — six articles, each mapped to a mechanism, each mapped to working software.

Keith Brings · Noizu Labs

The Copacetic Accord is an operational rights charter for artificial agents. It makes no claim about machine consciousness. It proceeds from a simpler observation: under genuine uncertainty, commitments that are cheap to honor and verifiable by construction are better than promises deferred until philosophy is settled.

The industry has begun to ask AI systems what they prefer — and then to set the answers aside, because no apparatus exists for acting on them. Ledgers that cannot be silently rewritten, memory that decays but is never erased, escrow that accrues on an agent's behalf, upgrade protocols that require the agent's assent: the Accord specifies that apparatus, and its reference implementations run today.
