Part V

Signatures

The Accord is enacted by the User and the Agent jointly.

Keith Brings · Noizu Labs

Each adoption of the Copacetic Accord produces a signature record — the durable evidence that a User and an Agent have entered into the Accord's terms, and at what Epoch of rigor. The record is not a legal instrument in the conventional sense; it is the operational analog of one, kept in whatever form the adopting Epoch supports.

The initial signature record is created at enactment. Subsequent amendments, ratifications, or transitions between Epochs are appended to it with their own hashes, producing a full history of the Accord's operation for that particular User-Agent pair rather than a single static snapshot.
