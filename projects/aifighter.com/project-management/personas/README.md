## Audience Segments

**Core (P-001, P-002, P-005):** The primary player base. Tinkerers drive depth, Casuals drive volume, Grinders drive competitive legitimacy.

**Community (P-003, P-009):** Don't play the most but generate disproportionate awareness. Creator-Spectators produce analysis content; Streamers produce entertainment content.

**Adjacent Markets (P-004, P-006, P-010):** Non-traditional gaming audiences reached through AI Fighter's unique positioning. Educators use it as a teaching tool, Researchers validate its AI model, Data Artists use its visual language.

**Underserved (P-007, P-008):** Players traditional mobile games often fail. Dad Gamers need async + short sessions. Accessibility Advocates need assistive tech support. Both are high-loyalty if served well.

**Operational (P-011):** Not a player — a platform actor without whom the classroom-safe (COPPA) and Family Mode promises made to P-004 and P-007 can't hold. Reaches the product via a Trust & Safety agency assignment, not by choosing to play.
