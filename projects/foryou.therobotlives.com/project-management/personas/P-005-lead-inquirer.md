# P-005: Tom Alvarez

| Attribute | Value |

| Occupation | Operations director at a mid-size manufacturer |

| Tech comfort | medium |

## Bio
Tom found noizu.com and wants to discuss a possible engagement. He is not a
subscriber — he is a lead. He wants to describe his project (company, type of
work, budget, timeline) in one contact submission and trust that a human will
follow up.

## Goals
- Send a rich inquiry describing his company, need, budget, and timeline.
- Get a clear confirmation that his message was received.
- Not be forced to create an account just to make contact.

## Frustrations
- Bare name+email contact forms that can't convey scope or budget.
- No confirmation, leaving him unsure the message went through.
- Contact flows that demand signup before they'll take a message.

## Behaviors
- Fills forms thoroughly when the fields are relevant to his ask.
- Skips optional fields that don't apply.
- Judges a firm's professionalism by its intake experience.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I want to hire the team, I want to describe my project's company, type,
> budget, and timeline in one message, so I get a relevant, prompt follow-up."

## Relationship to Product
Drives the Inquiries & Lead Capture epic and the noizu.com contact-form
enhancement (plan item 4): optional Company, Project type/service, Budget range,
Timeline atop name + email + free-text inquiry.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Rich inquiry — submits the enhanced noizu.com contact form with
  company, project type, budget, timeline.
- **Scenario 2:** Minimal inquiry — submits only name, email, and message when the
  extras don't apply.
- **Scenario 3:** Confirmation — receives an on-screen and/or email acknowledgment.
