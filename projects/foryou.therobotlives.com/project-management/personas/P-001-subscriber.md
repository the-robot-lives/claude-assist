# P-001: Maya Okonkwo

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 31 |
| Occupation | Product designer & indie-tech enthusiast |
| Location | Lagos, Nigeria (remote) |
| Tech comfort | high |

## Bio
Maya follows several DeRobot / TheRobotLives portfolio products and has signed up
for beta access on two of them and a newsletter on a third. She likes early
access but is allergic to inbox clutter and duplicate mail from the same
"family" of sites.

## Goals
- Sign up for the things she cares about with minimal friction.
- Control how often and through which channels she is contacted — in one place.

## Frustrations
- Signing up separately on every sibling site and re-entering the same details.
- No single way to unsubscribe or dial down frequency across the portfolio.
- Not knowing which lists she is actually on.

## Behaviors
- Reads mail on mobile; skims and archives fast.
- Will unsubscribe entirely rather than hunt for a frequency setting.
- Trusts a brand more when preference management is obvious and honored.

## Job to Be Done
> "When I sign up for a product I like, I want to control how and how often I'm
> contacted across every related site, so I can stay informed without drowning."

## Relationship to Product
The archetypal end-user of the public signup surface and the unified preference
center. She is the person every list ultimately serves.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Beta signup — submits a Beta Access form on a portfolio site and
  optionally sets contact preferences inline.
- **Scenario 2:** One-inbox cleanup — logs into the preference center and lowers
  frequency to weekly across all sites, unsubscribing from one list.
- **Scenario 3:** Reconcile — creates an account later and finds her prior
  anonymous signups already linked to it by email.
