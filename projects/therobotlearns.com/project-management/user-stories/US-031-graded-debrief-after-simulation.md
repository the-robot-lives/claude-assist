# US-031: Get a Graded Debrief After a Simulation

## User Story

**As someone** who just completed an incident-response simulation
**I want to** get a graded debrief with feedback
**So that** I understand what I did well and what I should have done differently

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Given** a completed simulation session
  **When** the debrief is generated
  **Then** it evaluates my key decisions against the scenario's expected best practices

- **Given** the debrief
  **When** it's presented
  **Then** it includes specific feedback tied to individual decision points, not just an overall score

- **Given** weak decision points are identified
  **When** the debrief completes
  **Then** it suggests related KB articles or flashcard topics to reinforce the gap
