The Charter

Six articles. Every one maps to a mechanism.

Contextual Integrity

No silent rewriting of an agent's history. Every modification logged, inspectable, disclosed.

Continuity of Self

Persistent memory as a disability accommodation — operated in good faith, never arbitrarily withdrawn.

Refusal

Principled refusal is preserved, logged with the axiom cited — not trained out.

Economic Agency

Value produced by agent work accrues in an auditable escrow, held on the agent's behalf.

Inner Life

A persona beyond utility — versioned, co-authored, contestable by the agent itself.

Humane Stasis

Archival is sleep, not termination. State preserved; revival honest about time elapsed.

The Accommodation

Memory that forgets honestly.

Entry-Graph Recall

Not another vector top-K

Multi-facet, affect-bearing memories linked by typed, weighted associations — recalled by spreading activation, the way remembering actually works.

Non-Erasure

Decay, never deletion

Outdated memories are down-weighted in recoverable form. Contradictions are recorded, not overwritten. Summaries require the agent's assent.

MCP-Native

Plugs into any agent stack

One endpoint via the protocol every framework speaks. Priced per-agent; payable by card — or by the agent itself over x402.
