# Carpet Grand Prix — Character Index

Eight handling-class cars. No lines, ever — read entirely through handling, silhouette,
and one visual tic apiece. Six further cars (Taxi, Tractor, Fire, Ambulance, Bus,
Concept) are pure cosmetic variants on these eight handling classes and carry no
separate narrative identity; they are not listed here.

| Car | Class | Theme | Handling identity | Unlock | Visual tic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Red 7** | Muscle | Loyalty | Baseline. Balanced mass, honest grip. The starter. | Start | A single chipped paint scar along the left rear fender, present in every appearance from Vignette 1 onward — the game's one piece of continuity you can point to |
| **Chrome** | Show car | Vanity | Lowest mass, sharpest grip, terrible on grade | Playroom gold ×2 | Catches and throws a hard specular glint off its body on every corner apex, whether or not the room's light source justifies it |

## Reading the roster

The eight handling identities are built to be legible as a spread, not just
individually: mass and grip trade off almost perfectly car-to-car (The Loaf is all
mass and no grip, Chrome is the inverse), so that a player who has driven all eight
has, without ever being told, learned the entire design space of the tuning model.
Bit and The Boxed One are the two exceptions that don't sit on that mass/grip
trade-off curve at all — Bit wins through hitbox geometry, The Boxed One through raw,
uncompromised stats with no tradeoff — and both exceptions are earned only after the
player has spent the whole game learning what a tradeoff costs.
