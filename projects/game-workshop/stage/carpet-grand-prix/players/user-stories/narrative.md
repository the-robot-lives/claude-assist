# Carpet Grand Prix — User Stories: Narrative & World (US-021 – US-025)

### US-021: Wordless Story
- **Persona:** P-002 (Sarah Chen)
- **Category:** Narrative & World
- **Priority:** Must
- **Story:** As Sarah, I want the story told without text, so that I can share the game with a five-year-old and a grandparent equally.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** All 9 vignettes contain no on-screen text, subtitles, or narration. Story beats are legible from visuals and music alone in an internal comprehension test across at least two age groups.

### US-022: Rooms Look and Sound Distinct
- **Persona:** P-002 (Sarah Chen)
- **Category:** Narrative & World
- **Priority:** Must
- **Story:** As Sarah, I want each new room to look and sound meaningfully different from the last, so that progressing feels like moving through a real house.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Each of the 7 rooms ships a unique floor material, palette, light source, and audio bed per the world-design visual/audio progression table. No two rooms share a floor texture or dominant light source.

### US-023: Music Accumulates by Layer
- **Persona:** Player (general)
- **Category:** Narrative & World
- **Priority:** Should
- **Story:** As a player, I want the music to gain a layer per room rather than switch tracks, so that later rooms feel like an accumulation of everything before.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** The score is authored as a single 7-part arrangement. Each room unlock adds exactly one stem to the mix; the Garden plays all 7 stems, the Playroom plays only the first.

### US-024: Track Casts a Real Shadow
- **Persona:** Player (general)
- **Category:** Narrative & World
- **Priority:** Should
- **Story:** As a player, I want the track to visibly cast a shadow onto the actual floor beneath it, so that the diorama reads as a physical object in a room.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Every room's dominant practical light source casts a fixed-direction shadow from the deck and posts onto the rendered floor texture. Shadow direction is consistent within a room and changes between rooms.

### US-025: Vignette Per Room Completion
- **Persona:** Player (general)
- **Category:** Narrative & World
- **Priority:** Must
- **Story:** As a player, I want a story vignette after completing each room, so that finishing a room has a payoff beyond the unlock.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Completing a room's unlock threshold plays one of the 9 vignettes (one per room, plus two at the story's end) automatically on next launch or immediately if triggered mid-session, without requiring the player to seek it out in a menu.
