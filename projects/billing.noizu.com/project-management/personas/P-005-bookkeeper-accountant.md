## Bio
Mateo is not in the tool every day, but he depends on exports and audit trails during tax preparation and reconciliation. He expects totals, tax treatment, and adjustments to be traceable.

## Goals
- Reconcile billing activity against bank and Stripe data
- Review tax, discount, refund, and credit summaries
- Import clean customer and invoice exports

## Frustrations
- Operational billing systems often lack immutable audit history
- Credit notes and refunds are hard to trace to original invoices
- Exports omit IDs needed for reconciliation

## Behaviors
- Requests exports monthly or quarterly
- Looks for stable invoice IDs and event timestamps
- Raises questions when state changes are not auditable

## Job to Be Done
> "When I review a billing period, I want complete exportable records with audit events, so I can reconcile books and explain exceptions."

## Relationship to Product
Tertiary reviewer who drives export, audit, tax, and reconciliation requirements.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Download monthly revenue and tax exports
- **Scenario 2:** Trace a credit note back to an invoice
