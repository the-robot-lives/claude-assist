## Bio
Owen tracks milestones, estimates, change requests, and client approvals. He does not own finance, but his work determines what becomes billable and how invoice line items should be explained.

## Goals
- Package approved work into billable milestones
- Convert estimates into invoices without losing scope history
- Give finance context before invoices go out

## Frustrations
- Billing teams ask for delivery context after the fact
- Scope revisions are hard to reconcile with the final invoice
- Client-facing summaries take too long to assemble

## Behaviors
- Reviews project status before invoice drafts
- Attaches notes, milestone IDs, and service periods
- Flags invoice drafts for finance review

## Job to Be Done
> "When a milestone is approved, I want to hand off billable context to finance in one structured flow, so invoices are accurate and client-ready."

## Relationship to Product
Secondary internal contributor who feeds invoice creation and estimate workflows.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Convert accepted estimate revisions into an invoice
- **Scenario 2:** Attach project context to a draft invoice
