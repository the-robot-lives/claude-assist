# P-001: Maya Rivera

| Attribute | Value |

| Location | Austin, TX |

## Bio
Maya runs a small Noizu-operated consulting practice and owns revenue hygiene directly. She needs fast invoice creation, a clear receivables picture, and enough automation that billing does not consume delivery time.

## Goals
- Send accurate invoices within minutes of work completion
- Know which customers owe money and what requires follow-up
- Avoid accounting-suite complexity while preserving auditability

## Frustrations
- Invoice details live across email, time tracking, spreadsheets, and Stripe
- Late payments are easy to miss until cash flow is already pinched
- Generic accounting tools hide project-delivery context

## Behaviors
- Checks revenue status several times per week
- Uses templates and copied line items to move quickly
- Delegates repetitive follow-up when risk is low

## Job to Be Done
> "When project work reaches a billable milestone, I want to turn delivery records into a polished invoice quickly, so I can collect revenue without breaking focus."

## Relationship to Product
Primary daily buyer-user for the MVP; validates whether Billing Noizu is faster than FreshBooks-style tools.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Create invoice from a project milestone
- **Scenario 2:** Review overdue invoices before weekly planning
