# P-008: Rina Kim

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 32 |
| Occupation | Client services lead |
| Location | Los Angeles, CA |
| Tech comfort | high |

## Bio
Rina is often away from her desk but still needs to review invoice drafts, approve reminders, and react to payment or overdue events. She expects mobile flows to be focused, fast, and safe enough for financial decisions on a small screen.

## Goals
- Review invoice and payment status from a phone
- Approve or reject drafted follow-ups without opening the full desktop app
- Receive timely alerts for overdue invoices, payments, and failed sends

## Frustrations
- Desktop-only billing tools block approvals when she is traveling
- Mobile web tables are hard to scan under time pressure
- Push notifications often lack enough context to act safely

## Behaviors
- Handles approvals between meetings
- Opens deep links from email, Slack, and push notifications
- Defers complex edits to desktop but expects simple decisions on mobile

## Job to Be Done
> "When a billing decision needs quick review, I want a focused native mobile flow, so I can keep revenue moving without making a careless financial change."

## Relationship to Product
Rina drives iOS and Android app requirements: mobile approval, concise status, push notifications, offline-tolerant reads, and safe confirmation flows.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Approve an invoice follow-up draft from a push notification
- **Scenario 2:** Check whether a client paid before joining a call
