## Bio
Nadia manages customer records, invoice status, payments, and monthly close for several small entities. She values precise status, clean exports, and recoverable workflows over flashy automation.

## Goals
- Maintain accurate receivables and payment history
- Export clean billing data for bookkeeping and taxes
- Spot exceptions before month-end close

## Frustrations
- Partial payments and credits are often misrepresented
- PDFs and payment records drift apart
- CSV exports rarely match accounting categories

## Behaviors
- Works in batches near billing cycles
- Uses filters, saved views, and exports heavily
- Double-checks anything that affects balances

## Job to Be Done
> "When invoices, payments, or credits change, I want the ledger-facing state to remain explainable, so I can close books without reconstructing history."

## Relationship to Product
Primary operations persona for payment tracking, credits, exports, and admin controls.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Record a wire payment against an invoice
- **Scenario 2:** Export paid invoices and payments for the accountant
