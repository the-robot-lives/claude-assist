## Bio
Clara receives invoices from Noizu vendors and pays them through company approval processes. She needs invoice emails and PDFs that answer common AP questions without requiring back-and-forth.

## Goals
- Understand what an invoice covers and when it is due
- Forward invoices internally with all payment details intact
- Pay by card or ACH when allowed

## Frustrations
- Invoices omit PO numbers, tax IDs, or service periods
- Payment links do not clarify partial payment rules
- Follow-up emails lack context

## Behaviors
- Searches email for invoice numbers and vendor names
- Downloads PDFs for internal approval systems
- Asks for corrected invoices when details are ambiguous

## Job to Be Done
> "When I receive an invoice, I want clear payment terms and supporting context, so I can route it for approval and payment without delays."

## Relationship to Product
External recipient whose needs shape invoice rendering, email delivery, payment links, and client portal UX.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Open an invoice email and download the PDF
- **Scenario 2:** Pay an invoice through a hosted payment link
