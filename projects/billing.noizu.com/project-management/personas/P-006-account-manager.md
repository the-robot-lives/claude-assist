# P-006: Leah Brooks

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |

| Occupation | Client account manager |
| Location | New York, NY |

## Bio
Leah prepares for client calls and handles relationship-sensitive follow-ups. She needs billing history and AI-drafted messages that are accurate, polite, and easy to edit.

## Goals
- Review a client billing snapshot before calls
- Send tactful overdue reminders with context
- Understand payment risk without reading the full ledger

## Frustrations
- Collections language can damage client relationships
- Billing history is split from support and project notes
- She cannot easily see whether a reminder was already sent

## Behaviors
- Reads summaries before account meetings
- Customizes email drafts before sending
- Coordinates with finance on sensitive accounts

## Job to Be Done
> "When a client account has billing activity or risk, I want a concise history and editable follow-up draft, so I can protect the relationship while collecting revenue."

## Relationship to Product
Tertiary collaboration persona for customer summaries, reminders, and activity history.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario 1:** Generate a polite overdue follow-up email
- **Scenario 2:** Review billing activity before a renewal call
