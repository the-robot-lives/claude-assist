# P-002: Owen Patel

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|

| Occupation | Operations Manager, B2B service company |

## Bio
Owen coordinates day-to-day workflows for a team doing recurring work across project onboarding, status updates, and quality checks. He is accountable for throughput and risk.

## Goals
- Enforce process consistency across repeated tasks.
- Spot blockers before they impact customers.
- Keep escalation routing clear for high-risk outputs.

## Frustrations
- Siloed status updates.
- Hard to compare work quality across teams and time periods.
- Too many "one-off automations" with no governance.

## Behaviors
- Tracks KPIs and exception queues.
- Uses review queues and audit logs in team meetings.
- Prefers explicit permissions over hidden defaults.

## Job to Be Done
> When recurring process steps shift across teammates, I want auditable robot workflows and clear handoff paths, so I can keep operations steady during churn.

## Relationship to Product
Primary for operations and workflow quality: he validates whether the product can replace his ad-hoc manual processes.

## Scenarios
- **Onboarding queue jam:** He shifts a stalled set of new-client tasks to a different robot team with zero-loss handoff history.
- **Incident day:** He flags an escalation condition and routes flagged outputs to a human specialist.
