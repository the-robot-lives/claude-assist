## Bio
Noor works with legal and security teams to define acceptable use for AI support and advisory workflows in regulated contexts.

## Goals
- Enforce auditability and explainability.
- Ensure role boundaries prevent unauthorized actions.
- Keep policy exceptions visible during escalation.

## Frustrations
- Opaque logs and incomplete provenance.
- Weak policy mapping across robot role changes.
- Poor traceability of tool calls and approvals.

## Behaviors
- Reviews policy checklists and exception flows.
- Requires logs exported in consistent format.
- Relies on permission settings to contain risk.

## Job to Be Done
> When AI workflows touch regulated outputs, I want role-based restrictions and complete trails, so I can approve usage and manage risk.

## Relationship to Product
Ensures trust framing, governance language, and controls are productized early, not added later.

## Scenarios
- **Incident test:** Noor validates that a redaction policy is enforced before outputs can be sent externally.
- **Quarter audit:** She exports role permissions, message chains, and human approvals to an internal evidence pack.
