# P-001: Mara Ortiz

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 36 |
| Occupation | Solo founder of 3-person AI services company |
| Location | Austin, TX |
| Tech comfort | high |

## Bio
Mara runs a micro-operations-heavy startup. She has one product, one engineering lead, and recurring operational tasks piling up across support, research, and reporting.

## Goals
- Reduce manual routine work without losing final accountability.
- Keep everything visible in one place.
- Move quickly between experiments on pricing and agent behavior.

## Frustrations
- Too much context switching across Slack, docs, and spreadsheets.
- Hard to trust AI-generated outputs without transparent reasoning trails.
- Hiring people to own repeatable work is expensive and slow.

## Behaviors
- Makes decisions daily on data dashboards.
- Approves or rejects work quickly and wants low-friction controls.
- Favors clear naming, clean status states, and quick summaries.

## Job to Be Done
> When recurring operational work grows, I want AI workers with defined roles and checklists, so I can keep quality high while scaling operations.

## Relationship to Product
Primary early adopter. She is the first person who needs a "team of robots" view that is easy to understand before she scales adoption.

## Scenarios
- **Quarter-close stress week:** Mara launches a weekly support triage workflow for 3 channels and keeps human review only for high-risk tickets.
- **New offer launch:** She re-runs the same planning + execution workflow with a stricter review gate and gets a faster time-to-delivery.
