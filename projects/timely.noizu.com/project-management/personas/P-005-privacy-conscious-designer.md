# P-005: Sofia Marin

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 29 |
| Occupation | Brand and product designer |
| Location | Madrid, Spain |
| Tech comfort | medium |

## Bio
Sofia works on confidential client designs and personal projects on the same machine. She can benefit from time evidence, but only if she controls capture, blur, retention, and sharing.

## Goals
- Track time without exposing sensitive visuals
- Pause or redact quickly
- Share only selected evidence with clients

## Frustrations
- Screenshot tools capture private chats and unreleased work
- Privacy settings are hard to understand
- Managers over-collect data because defaults allow it

## Behaviors
- Uses design tools, messaging apps, and browser research heavily
- Deletes sensitive screenshots
- Prefers clear consent and retention language

## Job to Be Done
> "When I need proof of work but handle confidential material, I want precise capture controls, so I can use time tracking without compromising trust."

## Relationship to Product
Edge-case daily user. Sofia drives privacy, redaction, and consent requirements.

## Scenarios
- **First week setup:** Configures projects, capture rules, and privacy defaults before trusting the tool.
- **Weekly review:** Uses timeline evidence to resolve ambiguity and close the week.
- **Exception handling:** Encounters idle gaps, overlap, or sensitive screenshots and needs fast correction.
