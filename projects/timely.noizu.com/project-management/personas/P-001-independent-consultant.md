# P-001: Maya Chen

| Attribute | Value |

## Bio
Maya bills across three retainer clients and two fixed-scope projects. Her day is fragmented by code review, incident support, client calls, and implementation work, and she needs defensible time records without babysitting timers.

## Goals
- Produce accurate client invoices with evidence
- Recover task context quickly after interruptions
- Separate billable work from admin overhead

## Frustrations
- Manual timers drift from reality after interruptions
- Screenshot tools feel invasive or hard to redact
- Parallel support and build work is hard to represent

## Behaviors
- Uses keyboard shortcuts heavily
- Reviews invoices weekly
- Often leaves long-running tasks active while handling quick support pings

## Job to Be Done
> "When my day splinters across clients, I want Timely to reconstruct intervals and evidence, so I can invoice confidently without losing evening hours to cleanup."

## Relationship to Product
Primary buyer and daily power user. Maya values fast correction flows, reliable screenshots, and nuanced overlapping task support.

## Scenarios
- **First week setup:** Configures projects, capture rules, and privacy defaults before trusting the tool.
- **Weekly review:** Uses timeline evidence to resolve ambiguity and close the week.
- **Exception handling:** Encounters idle gaps, overlap, or sensitive screenshots and needs fast correction.
