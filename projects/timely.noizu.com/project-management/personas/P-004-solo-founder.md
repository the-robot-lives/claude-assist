## Bio
Andre splits his day between product, support, sales, marketing, and operations. He wants a brutally accurate mirror of where time goes and whether focus blocks are real.

## Goals
- See where the day actually went
- Protect deep work blocks
- Tag work by company objective

## Frustrations
- End-of-day memory is unreliable
- Tasks overlap with support and sales follow-up
- Existing apps are either too simple or too managerial

## Behaviors
- Reviews daily summaries at night
- Creates many tiny tasks
- Uses calendar blocking but frequently deviates

## Job to Be Done
> "When I feel busy but progress is fuzzy, I want Timely to show the real allocation of attention, so I can adjust the next day with facts."

## Relationship to Product
Power user for self analytics, resumption prompts, and lightweight review.

## Scenarios
- **First week setup:** Configures projects, capture rules, and privacy defaults before trusting the tool.
- **Weekly review:** Uses timeline evidence to resolve ambiguity and close the week.
- **Exception handling:** Encounters idle gaps, overlap, or sensitive screenshots and needs fast correction.
