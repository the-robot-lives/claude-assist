## Bio
Leah leads a platform team with on-call rotations, meetings, code work, and production incidents. She wants to understand context switching and recovery cost without measuring people by keystrokes.

## Goals
- Understand interruption load across the team
- Separate incident work from planned roadmap work
- Use time data to improve planning

## Frustrations
- Calendar data hides real work
- Status reports omit unplanned support
- Tracking tools create trust problems

## Behaviors
- Looks at weekly trends, not minute-level surveillance
- Uses Jira, GitHub, Slack, and incident tools
- Cares about opt-in transparency

## Job to Be Done
> "When planned work slips because interrupts keep landing, I want evidence of attention load, so I can change process instead of blaming individuals."

## Relationship to Product
Team stakeholder. Leah uses analytics, team policies, and integrations more than personal screenshots.

## Scenarios
- **First week setup:** Configures projects, capture rules, and privacy defaults before trusting the tool.
- **Weekly review:** Uses timeline evidence to resolve ambiguity and close the week.
- **Exception handling:** Encounters idle gaps, overlap, or sensitive screenshots and needs fast correction.
