# P-008: Grace Kim

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|

| Occupation | Security and compliance consultant |
| Location | San Francisco, CA |
| Tech comfort | high |

## Bio
Grace evaluates whether tracking tools respect privacy, data retention, and client confidentiality requirements. She needs policy clarity and tamper-evident audit trails.

## Goals
- Confirm capture policies are enforceable
- Review access to screenshots and exports
- Verify retention and deletion behavior

## Frustrations
- Tools bury privacy behavior in vague settings
- Audit trails omit who changed what
- Retention policies are inconsistent across teams

## Behaviors
- Reviews settings before rollout
- Requests evidence during audits
- Cares about least-privilege permissions

## Job to Be Done
> "When a team wants screenshot-backed tracking, I want policy and audit controls, so the organization can use evidence without creating a compliance liability."

## Relationship to Product
Governance reviewer. Grace shapes admin settings, retention, permissions, and audit logging.

## Scenarios
- **First week setup:** Configures projects, capture rules, and privacy defaults before trusting the tool.
- **Weekly review:** Uses timeline evidence to resolve ambiguity and close the week.
- **Exception handling:** Encounters idle gaps, overlap, or sensitive screenshots and needs fast correction.
