# P-006: Nadia Okafor

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 47 |
| Occupation | Bookkeeper for a consulting firm |
| Location | Atlanta, GA |
| Tech comfort | medium |

## Bio
Nadia receives approved time records and turns them into invoices and accounting entries. She rarely tracks her own time but needs clean exports, predictable codes, and an audit path.

## Goals
- Export approved time cleanly
- Resolve invoice disputes quickly
- Map projects to accounting systems

## Frustrations
- Timesheets arrive with inconsistent task names
- Evidence is scattered across tools
- Corrections after invoicing are painful

## Behaviors
- Works in batches around billing cycles
- Uses CSV, QuickBooks, and spreadsheets
- Asks managers for clarification on exceptions

## Job to Be Done
> "When timesheets are approved, I want clean invoice-ready data with evidence links, so billing closes without manual cleanup."

## Relationship to Product
Back-office user. Nadia shapes reporting, approval state, export, and audit-log needs.

## Scenarios
- **First week setup:** Configures projects, capture rules, and privacy defaults before trusting the tool.
- **Weekly review:** Uses timeline evidence to resolve ambiguity and close the week.
- **Exception handling:** Encounters idle gaps, overlap, or sensitive screenshots and needs fast correction.
