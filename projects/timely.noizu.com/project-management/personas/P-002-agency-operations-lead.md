# P-002: Owen Patel

## Demographics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Age | 42 |
| Occupation | Director of operations at a 14-person digital agency |
| Location | Austin, TX |
| Tech comfort | medium |

## Bio
Owen coordinates designers, developers, and account managers across fixed-bid and time-and-material contracts. He needs utilization visibility without turning the team into surveillance subjects.

## Goals
- See project burn and staffing risk early
- Approve timesheets quickly
- Enforce privacy-safe capture rules

## Frustrations
- Late timesheets create invoice delays
- Team members resist tools that feel punitive
- Project managers argue over ambiguous task categories

## Behaviors
- Reviews dashboards Monday morning
- Exports reports to accounting
- Defines client-specific billing policies

## Job to Be Done
> "When project hours are scattered across the team, I want high-confidence summaries and exception review, so I can invoice and staff work without micromanaging."

## Relationship to Product
Economic buyer and admin user. Owen needs policy controls, team reporting, and audit trails.

## Scenarios
- **First week setup:** Configures projects, capture rules, and privacy defaults before trusting the tool.
- **Weekly review:** Uses timeline evidence to resolve ambiguity and close the week.
- **Exception handling:** Encounters idle gaps, overlap, or sensitive screenshots and needs fast correction.
